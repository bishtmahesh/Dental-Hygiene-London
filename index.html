What Is Direct-Access Dental Hygiene?

Direct access allows you to book an appointment with a qualified dental hygienist without first seeing a dentist.

Your hygienist will assess your gum health, provide appropriate professional cleaning and offer personalised advice to help maintain your oral health.

A hygiene appointment does not replace a comprehensive dental examination. If your hygienist identifies concerns requiring a dentist, they will explain the findings and recommend further assessment.

Direct-access hygiene appointments focus on professional cleaning and gum care. They do not replace a comprehensive dental examination.

Existing fillings, crowns, veneers, bridges and other dental work may require separate assessment or treatment by a dentist.

Any additional dental examinations, X-rays, repairs or restorative treatment are not included in the hygiene appointment fee and will be charged separately. Read the full details in our FAQs

FAQs

Questions about seeing a hygienist

Do I need to see a dentist first?

No. You can book directly with a hygienist, even if you haven’t seen a dentist in years. They’ll carry out a gum health screening first and may recommend a dentist depending on what they find. It’s a hygiene appointment rather than a full dental examination, so it doesn’t replace routine check-ups. If an examination would help, your hygienist will let you know and can help you arrange it.

What does the appointment include?

A gum health screening, your professional clean and oral hygiene advice. Stain removal is included where it’s clinically appropriate, and you can book just to have stains removed if your hygienist confirms it’s suitable. Photos or a digital scan may be used, where appropriate, to show you what they’ve seen.

How much does it cost, and can I claim on insurance?

Our professional cleans have fixed prices: Standard Professional Clean £85, Standard Plus Professional Clean £115 (with professional air polishing) and Enhanced Professional Clean £245. Examinations, X-rays and any repair or restorative treatment are charged separately at the relevant dental fee. If your needs are more extensive than expected, for example heavier calculus or inflamed gums, you could need more than one appointment. Your hygienist will discuss it with you first, and extra or longer appointments are charged separately. Patients with eligible dental insurance may be able to claim for hygiene treatment, depending on their policy. You pay us, and we email you a receipt to claim back. Please check your own policy, as cover varies.

What’s the difference between a professional hygiene clean and a deep clean?

A professional hygiene clean (scale and polish) is a routine clean above and just below the gum line, for healthy gums or mild inflammation. A deep clean (scaling and root planing) cleans several millimetres into gum pockets under local anaesthetic, for established gum disease. Your hygienist will tell you which you need.

What should I expect after my clean, and how often should I come back?

Some sensitivity after a clean is common and temporary, and your hygienist can suggest what helps. The effects last several months if you keep up a good routine at home: brush and clean between your teeth twice a day, and cut back on sugar, fizzy and acidic drinks, and smoking. Your hygienist will show you the right techniques and products. We recommend a hygienist appointment at least every six months, as regular cleans remove the build-up of plaque and tartar that can lead to gum disease.

What about my existing fillings, crowns and other dental work?

Fillings, crowns, veneers, bridges, implant restorations, bonded retainers and other dental work can already be loose, damaged or compromised, often without symptoms. Hygiene treatment can sometimes reveal or dislodge this. If something comes loose, your hygienist will explain what has happened and the next step. If something already looks weak, they may recommend a dentist checks it before that area is cleaned. Repair, re-cementation, replacement or investigation of existing dental work isn’t covered by the hygiene fee, and any examination, X-rays or restorative treatment is booked separately at the relevant dental fee.

What will you need from me before my appointment?

We’ll ask for your medical history, relevant dental information and a signed consent form. Please tell us about any loose or damaged fillings, crowns, veneers, bridges, implants or bonded retainers, recent dental treatment, sensitivity, pain or swelling.

What if I need to cancel or change my appointment?

Please give us at least 2 working days’ notice if you need to cancel or change your hygiene appointment. Late cancellations, changes and missed hygiene appointments may be charged the full appointment fee. If a deposit was taken when you booked, it’s refunded when you cancel with more than 2 working days’ notice. We always try to be understanding about personal circumstances. Read our full cancellation policy.

What if I have pain, swelling or a broken tooth?

Please call us on 020 7928 4474 before booking. These problems need a dentist rather than a hygiene appointment, and our team can help you arrange one.
